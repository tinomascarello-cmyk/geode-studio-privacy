Privacy Policy – Geode Studio
Last updated: November 21, 2026
Geode Studio is a graphic creation app. It runs entirely on your phone.
Data collected: none. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It contains no ads, no analytics and no user accounts.
Files. The images and saves you create are stored only on your phone (in the Documents/Geode Studio folder) or wherever you choose through Android's share menu. Images you import for a sculpture stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere.
Permissions. On Android 10 and earlier, the app may ask for storage access, solely to save your files in the Documents folder.
Contact. For any questions: tinomascarello@gmail.com.
